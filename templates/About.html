Why is it Needed?

Crime trends in India fluctuate dynamically with demographic growth and urban expansion. Traditional policing requires countless manual hours analyzing raw data logs.

By leveraging machine learning and geospatial mapping, our platform provides actionable inferences, predictive risk scores, and alerting systems to transform reactive policing into proactive crime prevention.

Who Benefits?

  • Law Enforcement Agencies: Allocate surveillance patrols to high-density Red Zones.
  • Researchers & Academics: Scraped news databases for empirical crime studies.
  • General Public: Real-time safety awareness and hotspot geographic alerts.
